# Full-height phone framing and daylight

The phone scene fills the complete viewport behind compact controls. Its
control footprint was measured against the actual web game: full 390 × 844
canvas, 44 px actions, a 167 px working dock and ordered arrivals above the
board. Safe areas, short/tall screens, modal tools and chapter cards reserve
space through camera insets. The board's projection and authored data remain
unchanged. This is a review study, not a native or production UI integration.

Paper sky and world-aligned paving now cover the exposed edges at the widest
zoom. A stone approach continues from the arrival steps into the civic square.
Soft receiving shadows ground the plinth, buildings and galleries; warm wall
light and restrained distant façades add depth. Original skyline, material
masters, windows and scenery remain retained.

Eleven matched before/after views use identical corrected controls, cameras,
levels and ambient time. A separate comparison shows the old restricted phone
framing. The 32-second actual renderer clip shows the full phone scenery
without DOM controls.

Verification: 38 focused tests, 72 browser checks, 26 qualification checks,
44 phone checks and all 15 actual City Hall wins. All 195 protected files,
237 build inputs and three board rasters match their recorded hashes. Warm
draws allocate no new canvases; the new prepared background buffers are
included in the resource estimate and released on disposal.

The independent Astra review found no Critical or Important issues. Two Minor
rotation/restoration issues were reproduced and corrected, with focused
regressions passing. Whole-room artistic acceptance and physical-device/Safari
qualification remain open.

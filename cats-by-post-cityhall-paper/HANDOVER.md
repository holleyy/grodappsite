# The Great Postal Vault

City Hall playable study for all fifteen existing deliveries, 6-1 through 6-15.
Selected direction: the second height study, with the tall curved glass-card
vault. The later backdrop pass connects its plinth to a measured civic square:
six entrance steps, paving and kerbs, neighbouring buildings, trees, lamps and
fountain. One distant papercraft skyline remains registered through every window.
The board, pieces and nearby construction share the game's exact 2:1 projection.

Open [the playable study](playable.html). Explore hall shows the whole setting;
Return to board restores the closer view. Drag to pan; pinch or +/− to zoom.
Arrow keys select, Enter places/rotates, Space sends. Motion and G provide
reduced ambient motion and grayscale. All three ordered hatch queues remain
available in compact layouts. The study uses isolated save keys.

Bare cut-paper branches and dormant gardens sit among layered snow on roofs, ledges and wall caps; exposed slate and the walking paths keep the construction readable. Spring has smaller leaves and blossom, while summer has fuller green canopies and gardens. Night artwork carries the same changes under amber windows and indigo skies.

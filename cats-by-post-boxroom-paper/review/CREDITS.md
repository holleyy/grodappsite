# Review artwork and evidence

The original and final game captures depict Cats by Post project artwork. The original engine was verified against current main after review namespace/build-label normalisation; see evidence/baseline-equivalence.json.

The selected concept, material/carton atlases, town panels and collected-cat artwork were generated with Image Gen for this approved task, using the supplied Cats by Post references. Source masters and the preserved prompts are in studies/boxroom-paper/masters. Exact provenance, encoding and hashes are in src/fidelity/boxroom-paper/assets/provenance.json. Accepted Office acting pixels and the original optional-mechanism art are reused without pixel edits.

Gallery images are WebP encodings of the original browser captures or approved concept at their original resolution, without retouching. images.json records source and encoded hashes. Contact sheets were rendered as HTML in Chromium. Synthetic fixtures are labelled separately from actual controller play.

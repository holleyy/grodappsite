## What changed

surrounds, roof bearings/gussets, panelled sorting counter, weighing scale,
roller/lift/chute and tea station. The accepted loft remains intact. Supported
postal bicycles, kerbs, packages, a connected service road and rail context sit
within a warmer dawn backdrop. The clerk has a real stool/foot ring and pull-out
writing shelf, with a small reading nod while holding her original letter.

A pure room-owned adapter maps the original 2,880-frame Depot schedule to these
physical service points. The courier uses a complete two-view paper source,
clipped into cached limbs/shoes at load. Stance shoes stay fixed while the other
leg swings. Hands follow the same parcel, bag, cup or trolley handle through
transfers. The cat/parcel, scale dwell, labelled dispatch, independently finishing
parcel, bag exchange and tea cycle remain seekable and repeatable.

The canonical source has no van-motion track. The visual van departure and
return use the existing 48-second room loop and driver-clear windows; the van
remains parked during the original bag exchange. No new gameplay clock was
introduced. The original action/cargo state boundaries are preserved for every
frame. Presentation paths, service registration and pose interpolation are owned
by this study, rather than edits to source level or motion data.

Growth scenes keep their existing time and board. The cart has a reachable high
handle; its worker/cart draw in front of the board instead of being cut by its
foreground. The satchel exit retains the completed finale board. The chapter
controller, gameplay pieces, portals, projection and structural geometry remain
byte-identical to the accepted corner.
